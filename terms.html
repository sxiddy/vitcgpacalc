What this site is

VIT cgpa is a free, unofficial tool for VIT Vellore students to estimate their marks, grades, GPA and CGPA. It is not made by or linked to VIT.

Estimates only

Grades depend on the ranges you enter and on VIT's relative grading, so results are estimates. Always check your official results on VTOP. We aren't responsible for decisions made from these numbers.

Fair use

Sign in only with your own VIT account. Don't try to break, overload or misuse the site. We may limit or remove access that's being abused.

The site is provided as is and may change or go offline at any time. We may update these terms; the date above shows the latest version.
