Questions

Is this made by VIT?

No. It's an independent tool made by a VIT Vellore student. Your official marks and grades are always the ones on VTOP.

Why do I have to enter grade ranges?

VIT grades are relative, so the cut-offs change from class to class. Type in the ranges your faculty shares and the grade will match your class.

Are my marks saved?

Yes, on this device. Sign-in with your VIT student account is coming soon so your marks follow you to every device.

My subject isn't in the list. What now?

Add it yourself with its code, title, type and credits. It takes ten seconds and works exactly like the built-in ones.

How is CGPA worked out?

Credits × grade points for every course, divided by your total credits. Bigger semesters count more, so it's not a plain average of your GPAs.
