How it works

Three steps, under a minute.

01

Add your subjects

Search by course code or name. Theory, lab, theory + lab and online courses are all supported.

02

Enter your marks

CAT-1 out of 15, CAT-2 out of 15, FAT out of 40, quizzes out of 30 and your lab assignments.

03

See your grade and GPA

Enter your class's grade ranges and get your grade for each subject plus your semester GPA. Then add past semesters for your CGPA.

How your total is worked out

The same split VIT uses for theory + lab courses.

Theory · 75

CAT-1 + CAT-2 + FAT + quizzes

15 + 15 + 40 + 30 makes 100, which is scaled down to 75.

Lab · 25

Your lab assignments

Usually 10 assignments of 10 marks. Whatever the lab total is, it's scaled to 25.

Total · 100

Grade from your ranges

Ranges change from class to class, so you enter yours. Your grade and GPA follow from them.
