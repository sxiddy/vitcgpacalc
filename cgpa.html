      Cleared a failed course?

      When you pass a course you failed, the new grade replaces the F. If both attempts are in the calculator this happens on its own. If the F is inside a CGPA or GPA you typed in above, add the course's credits here so the old F stops counting. The new grade should be in a later semester.

        How CGPA is worked out

        CGPA = (credits × grade points for every course, across all semesters) ÷ (total credits). A semester with more credits counts more, so it isn't a plain average of your GPAs. An F gives 0 points but its credits still count, until you clear it.
